Shipping & Delivery

We deliver across Cambodia. Shipping is a flat $2.50 per order, and free for orders of $40 or more. Orders in Phnom Penh usually arrive within 1–2 working days; provincial orders take a little longer.

Returns & Refunds

If your book arrives damaged or isn't the one you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo, and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Terms & Conditions

Prices are shown in US dollars and may change without notice. By placing an order you confirm that the delivery details you provide are correct. Orders are confirmed once we've contacted you.

Privacy Policy

We only use the details you give us (name, phone, address and email) to process and deliver your order and to answer your messages. We don't sell your personal information.
